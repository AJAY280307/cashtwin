
# CashTwin

## Your AI Financial Twin

### Predict Early. Prevent Financial Crisis.

CashTwin is an AI-powered Financial Distress Prevention Platform designed to identify early signs of financial stress before they become a serious financial crisis.

The platform analyzes financial behavior, detects warning signals, explains potential financial risks, and provides personalized interventions to help users improve their financial stability.

---

## Problem Statement

### Preventing Financial Distress Before It Becomes a Crisis

How might banks responsibly identify early signs of financial distress and provide personalized interventions that help customers avoid excessive debt, loan defaults, and financial exclusion?

Financial problems often begin long before a missed payment or loan default.

Some early warning signs include:

* Declining account balances
* Increasing monthly expenses
* High EMI burden
* Declining savings
* High credit utilization
* Delayed payments
* Repeated overdrafts
* Increasing borrowing patterns

CashTwin aims to detect these signals early and help users take action before financial stress becomes a crisis.
